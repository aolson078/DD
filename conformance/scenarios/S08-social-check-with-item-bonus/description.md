# S08 - Social Check with Item Bonus

Validates modifier collection including equipped-item bonuses. A bard
(entity 1) with an Instrument of Charisma equipped attempts a Persuasion
check. Modifiers include proficiency, CHA stat bonus, the item's bonus,
and an active bardic inspiration die. The log shows the full modifier
breakdown and the final total matching the expected sum.

Exercises invariants: RE1 (modifier collection), RE2 (item modifier sourcing).
